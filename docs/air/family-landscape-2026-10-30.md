## Test-harness findings

Initial failed runs are retained. Adding the sixth app exposed a missing artwork label and an assumed static 404 entry; IMG intentionally routes unknown paths to its start page. First-cache notices can reflow or move an illustration offscreen. The artwork driver now waits for the visible hit target to stabilize and for IntersectionObserver to re-enable replay after scrolling, while retaining busy/held-press and offscreen assertions. The IMG overlay driver now waits for the new file-information dialog rather than inspecting the outgoing actions dialog. These corrections do not loosen application assertions or change runtime lifecycle behavior.

## Remaining acceptance

Physical iPhone/software keyboards, OS IME, spoken assistive output and hardware-specific acceptance remain open. Automated WebKit mobile layouts are not a physical Safari test. Existing PDF Compact-model long-document limitation and native release/signing/hardware gates remain separately tracked. No blanket “100% verified” claim is made beyond the enumerated web coverage.

CloakDrop has three pre-existing local native/release commits. Its web release is prepared from remote dev in an isolated checkout; those commits are not published by this task.
